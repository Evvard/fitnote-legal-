2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, phone number, username, password
  • Fitness Data: Workout logs, exercises, sets, reps, weight, duration, rest periods
  • Health Data: Body weight, height, age, gender, morphological profile (chest, waist, hips measurements) — optional
  • AI Coach Interactions: Messages sent to MAX (our AI coach) for form tips, motivation, and workout advice
  • Payment Information: Processed through RevenueCat (we do not store payment card details directly)

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifier
  • App Analytics: Features used, crash reports, app performance metrics
  • Advertising ID: Google Mobile Ads ID (for personalized advertising)
  • IP Address & Log Data: Timestamps, error logs, usage patterns

2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Precise location data (GPS, cellular location)
  • ❌ Contact lists, calendar, or file access
  • ❌ Biometric data (fingerprints, facial recognition)
  • ❌ Health app data (unless explicitly synced by user — not supported yet)

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

4.1 Service Providers (Data Processors)

  • Supabase (Backend): Stores user accounts, workout data. Servers in Europe (GDPR-compliant). Privacy Policy
  • Groq (AI Coaching): Processes MAX coach conversations. Requests are ephemeral (not stored by Groq). Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (Subscriptions): Handles in-app purchases, subscription management. Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Serves ads to free users. Collects advertising ID and device data. Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Analytics): Crash reporting and app performance. Privacy Policy

4.2 Data Sharing Policy

We do not sell your personal data. We share data with third parties only for the purposes listed above and only to the extent necessary.

Your workout and health data is never shared with advertisers or third parties for marketing purposes.

10. Advertising & Tracking

10.1 AdMob (Free Users)

Free users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob collects:

  • Device type, OS version, country
  • Approximate location (not precise GPS)
  • In-app behavior to personalize ads

You can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Manage your Google Account → Data & Privacy → Ad Personalization → Turn off

We do not use third-party tracking pixels, cookies, or SDKs for cross-app tracking.
